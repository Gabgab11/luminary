Founder & CEO, Luminary Digital Group

Gabriel Ayomitunde Uji

Gabriel Ayomitunde Uji, known online as Gabriel Uji and @mr_eyanu, is a Nigerian technology entrepreneur, software engineer and digital marketing specialist based in New York. Founder and CEO of Luminary Digital Group, helping businesses turn ideas into working digital products, then grow them.

About Gabriel

Software, product and growth under one roof

Gabriel's background combines software engineering, product development, digital marketing and business growth. He works across JavaScript and React engineering, UI/UX design, web application development and digital advertising.

As founder and CEO of Luminary Digital Group, he works with businesses, startups and entrepreneurs to build digital experiences, develop websites and applications, and grow their brands through Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads.

He has worked as a Senior JavaScript Software Engineer and as a Digital Marketing Manager, and his background spans digital solutions, branding, account management, UI/UX, data analytics and cybersecurity. He studied at The Polytechnic, Ibadan.

He enjoys building things from the ground up and solving practical problems with technology, especially scalable products that solve real-world problems and create meaningful business opportunities.
